IKIMINA SMART SYSTEM

Ikimina play an important role in promoting a culture of saving,
financial cooperation, and economic development among community members.
Through ikimina, people come together to save money, support one another financially,
access loans, and work toward achieving their personal and collective goals.
They also help members develop financial discipline, mutual trust, and a spirit of cooperation.

WHAT WE OFFER

Our Services

Ikimina Smart System provides digital solutions to help community savings groups manage their members, savings, loans, meetings, and financial activities efficiently.

Member Management

Register new members, update personal information, and manage group membership records.

Savings Management

Record members' savings, track contributions, and view savings history.

Loan Management

Record loan applications, monitor approved loans, track repayments, and calculate outstanding balances.

Financial Records

Organize income, expenses, payments, and other financial records in one place.

Reports & Analytics

Generate financial reports about savings, loans, repayments, and group performance.

Security & Transparency

Protect financial records and support transparent monitoring of group transactions.

Meeting Management

Schedule meetings, record attendance, and keep meeting minutes.

Penalty Management

Record penalties for late payments, missed meetings, and other agreed group rule violations.

Profit & Dividend Sharing

Calculate group profits and record the distribution of dividends according to group rules.

Notifications & Reminders

Send reminders about meetings, savings deadlines, loan repayments, and announcements.

Data Backup & Recovery

Back up important records and restore data from available backups when necessary.
